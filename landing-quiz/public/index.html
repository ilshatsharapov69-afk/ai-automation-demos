    FAQ

    Questions we hear before every project

    Can the price grow during the works?

    No. The total from the itemised estimate goes into the contract. If you ask for extra work, we agree it in writing first, as a separate line.

    Who buys the materials?

    Either we do, at cost with receipts and our trade discounts, or you do from our list. Most clients choose the first, it saves time.

    Do I need to be on site?

    No. You get a photo report every Friday and a chat with your foreman. Come to the stage acceptances, or accept them by video.

    Do you take on a single room or a bathroom?

    Yes. Pick the area of the room in the calculator and the extras you need.

    What does the warranty cover?

    All works for 3 years: wiring, plumbing, tiles, screed. If something goes wrong, we come and fix it at our cost.
